Where ecommerce profit usually leaks

VAT on platform fees

Marketplace and payment fees can carry UK VAT. Recorded as one net payout, it is never reclaimed.

Missing sales

Booking payouts as sales understates turnover, hides your real fee and ad costs, and distorts your VAT threshold check.

VAT registration timing

UK businesses must register over £90,000 taxable turnover in a rolling 12 months. Overseas sellers storing stock in the UK have no threshold.

Margin you can't see

Without settlements split by channel, you can't tell which marketplace, product or ad campaign actually makes money.
