How Much Does an Accountant Cost in London? (2026 Fixed Fees)

Fixed monthly fees for sole traders, limited companies and ecommerce sellers, what is included, and what pushes the price up or down.

Mubarik Mohamed, ACCA
· November 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer: at TechEdge, accountancy for a London sole trader starts at £75 a month, a limited company at £150 a month, and a growing business with monthly management accounts at £350 a month. A one-off Self Assessment tax return is £99. All prices are fixed, agreed before we start, and exclude VAT.

Most firms won't tell you their price until you've had a call. We think you should know roughly what you'll pay before you pick up the phone, so here's exactly how our fees work and what changes them.

What changes the price

Two businesses with the same turnover can need very different amounts of work. The things that move the fee are:

  • Transaction volume. A consultant with 20 invoices a month needs far less bookkeeping than an Amazon seller with thousands of orders.
  • VAT. Being VAT registered adds quarterly returns. Selling into the EU can add OSS returns.
  • Payroll. Directors only, or a team with pensions and benefits.
  • Number of sales channels. Each marketplace or payment provider needs its settlements reconciled.
  • The state of your books. Clean, up-to-date Xero books cost less to maintain than a year of bank statements in a shoebox.
  • How often you want numbers. Annual accounts only, or monthly management accounts you can run the business on.

Fixed fees vs hourly billing

We only work on fixed fees. Hourly billing makes you think twice before asking a question, and that is exactly when mistakes happen. With a fixed fee, questions by email or WhatsApp are included and you know your annual cost before you start.

What you shouldn't be paying for

  • Surprise invoices for "additional work" you never agreed
  • Being charged every time you ask a question
  • A year-end-only service where nobody looks at your numbers for 12 months
  • Paying for a qualified accountant but only ever speaking to junior staff

How to keep your accountancy bill down

  • Connect your bank and payment feeds to Xero so transactions arrive automatically
  • Snap receipts as you go rather than in a January panic
  • Keep personal and business spending in separate accounts
  • Fix messy past years once, properly, rather than paying for workarounds every year

Frequently asked questions

Are accountancy fees tax-deductible?

Fees for preparing your business accounts and dealing with your business tax are normally an allowable business expense, which reduces your taxable profit.

Do I legally need an accountant?

No. You can file your own Self Assessment or company accounts. Most people use an accountant to save time, avoid penalties and pay less tax legally.

Can I switch accountant part-way through the year?

Yes. We handle the handover with your current accountant and HMRC. See how to switch accountants in the UK.

Do you only work with London businesses?

We're based in London and work with clients across the UK. Everything runs online, so location doesn't affect the price.
